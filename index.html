Why Children Struggle to Read

There are many reasons children have a hard time reading. Here are some of the most common:

Phonics or Decoding Gaps

Difficulty sounding out words and recognizing patterns.

Weak Reading Comprehension

Can read the words but struggle to understand the meaning.

Low Confidence or Frustration

Past struggles can make children feel discouraged and avoid reading.

Attention or Focus Challenges

Can be hard to stay focused long enough to practice and improve.

Not Knowing How to Help at Home

Many parents want to help but aren’t sure what strategies work best.

Meet Dr. Nancy Barker

Dr. Nancy Barker has worked in education for over 40 years and brings a wealth of experience as a teacher, school leader, and former Assistant Superintendent. She holds three master’s degrees and an Ed.D., and has dedicated her career to helping children become stronger, more confident readers. Her work has impacted thousands of students, parents, and educators, and she continues to share practical resources that make a real difference in children’s lives.
